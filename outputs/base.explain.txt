8. WARNINGS AND LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  LIMIT: The model is additive on log prices with persistent item and supplier levels; if relative SKU prices drift within the six-month window the month effect is biased toward the average drift.
  LIMIT: Families only record a replacement relationship. Any old/new level difference reported here is a measured persistent level difference from overlap, not a quality or equivalence claim.
  LIMIT: Identification is exact and conditional on the observed quote pattern; absence of overlap is reported as insufficient support rather than bridged with external product prices.
  LIMIT: Supplier-level disturbance dependence is handled only by whole-trajectory resampling; no parametric covariance model is fitted.
  LIMIT: Percentile bootstrap intervals are descriptive and may be optimistic when the bridge rests on a single supplier, which is shown explicitly by the delete-one stability table.
  LIMIT: No forecasting, hedonic regression, rolling analysis or automatic product matching is performed.
