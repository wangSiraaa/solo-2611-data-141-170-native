  LIMIT: Families only record a replacement relationship. Any old/new level difference reported here is a measured persistent level difference from overlap, not a quality or equivalence claim.

  LIMIT: Supplier-level disturbance dependence is handled only by whole-trajectory resampling; no parametric covariance model is fitted.

  LIMIT: No forecasting, hedonic regression, rolling analysis or automatic product matching is performed.
